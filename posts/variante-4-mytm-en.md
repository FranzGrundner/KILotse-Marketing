Hey everyone 👋

I did the math last week: I used to spend **30–45 minutes every morning** just sorting
my work. Emails in Gmail, meetings in the calendar, notes on my phone, tasks scattered
everywhere — no overview, constant app-switching.

So I built myself something.

**MyTM** — my personal AI command center. Runs locally in the browser, every morning it does:

→ Fetches emails, Claude reads each one and suggests: delete / reply / save as task.
I just click yes or no.  
→ Calendar for the next 7 days right next to it  
→ Notes from my phone synced automatically  
→ All open tasks from all my projects on one page

Result: **5–10 minutes** instead of 30–45. Every single morning.
